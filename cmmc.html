Your CMMC level follows from the information in your environment. Pin that down first and everything else — scope, cost, timeline — gets clearer.

Level 1 — Foundational

For contractors that handle Federal Contract Information (FCI) but no CUI. 15 basic practices, verified by an annual self-assessment. Straightforward — but the score still has to be true.

The 2026 Pause

Verification is paused. Your obligations are not.

The DoD suspended CMMC's third-party assessment rollout in July 2026. Here's what that actually changes — and what it doesn't.

The DoD paused Phase 2 — the third-party assessment requirement — pending a program review. Some suppliers read that as permission to stop. It isn't.

DFARS 252.204-7012 and NIST 800-171 are still binding contract law. SPRS self-assessment scores are still required. And False Claims Act enforcement against inflated self-assessments is active and growing — the government doesn't need an assessment program to prosecute a score you can't back up.

Verification is expected to return. Companies that keep implementing win either way: ready if the requirement comes back, defensible if enforcement comes first. For the full picture, read our analysis of the pause.

FAQ

Common questions

Straight answers to the ones we hear most from defense suppliers.

Is CMMC dead now that Phase 2 is paused?

No. The DoD paused the third-party assessment requirement in July 2026 pending a program review — it did not pause your obligations. DFARS 252.204-7012 and NIST 800-171 remain binding contract law, SPRS self-assessment scores are still required, and verification is expected to return. Meanwhile, False Claims Act enforcement of inflated self-assessments is active and growing. The requirements didn't go anywhere. Only the check did.

We're a small manufacturer. Can we afford this?

Yes. We regularly work with small manufacturers and subcontractors and can phase work to align with budget and contract timelines.

Do you help fix what you find?

Yes. A gap assessment is the starting point, not the deliverable. We build the remediation plan, develop policies and your SSP, and can implement or manage controls ourselves — you decide how much stays in-house.

Will assessment work disrupt our operations?

No. Gap assessments run on interviews, documentation review, and read-only technical inspection, scheduled around your production. Where hands-on testing adds value, it's scoped, authorized, and timed in advance — nothing touches a production line without your sign-off.
