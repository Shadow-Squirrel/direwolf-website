FAQ

Common questions

The three things people ask before they reach out.

We're a small business with a small budget. Is Direwolf a fit?

Yes. We scope every engagement to the size and complexity of your environment — a small shop doesn't need, and shouldn't pay for, an enterprise-scale engagement. Tell us your budget up front and we'll tell you honestly what we can do within it.

Will you help us fix what you find?

Yes. Every finding comes with practical remediation guidance ranked by real-world risk. If you want help doing the work, we offer remediation planning, policy development, and ongoing support after the engagement ends.

Will testing disrupt our operations?

It shouldn't, and we plan so it doesn't. Every test runs under written rules of engagement: what's in scope, what's off-limits, and when testing happens. For production environments, we schedule around your operational windows and coordinate with your team throughout.
