Privacy Policy

This policy describes what information this website collects, how Direwolf Cybersecurity ("Direwolf," "we," "us") uses it, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect only what you choose to send us, we don't run trackers, and we don't sell your information.

Information we collect

The only personal information this website collects is what you submit through our contact form: your first and last name, work email address, and message. If you contact us directly by phone or email, we receive whatever information you choose to share.

This site has no user accounts, no comment system, and no newsletter signup.

How we use it

We use the information you send us to respond to your inquiry, scope and deliver services you request, and keep normal business records of our correspondence. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not add you to marketing lists without your consent.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. If that ever changes, we will update this policy first.

Service providers

Like most websites, we rely on a small number of service providers to operate this site:

  • Formspree processes contact-form submissions and delivers them to our email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. As with any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as visitor IP addresses for security and operational purposes.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces on this site. When a page loads, your browser requests font files from Google's servers, which discloses your IP address to Google.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. We share only what is necessary for these services to function — nothing more.

Data retention

We keep inquiry and correspondence records for as long as they serve a legitimate business purpose, such as responding to you, delivering services, or meeting legal obligations, and delete them when they no longer do.

Your choices

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email Contact@DirewolfCybersecurity.com and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Children

This website is intended for business audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We apply reasonable safeguards to the information we hold — it is, after all, our line of work. That said, no method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version here with a new effective date.
