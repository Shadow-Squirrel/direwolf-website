About Direwolf

Veteran-built, mission-driven

Direwolf Cybersecurity is a veteran-owned firm built for the companies that equip America's defense — to give them the security posture their mission deserves.

Why We Exist

Nation-critical work deserves serious defense

The suppliers who keep the defense industrial base running are exactly the ones adversaries target. That is who we built Direwolf to protect.

Small and mid-size manufacturers and defense suppliers carry nation-critical work on lean IT budgets. The parts, drawings, and data moving through your shop matter to national security — and adversaries know it. They don't only go after the primes; they go after the supply chain, where defenses are thinner and one compromised supplier opens a path to everything upstream.

Direwolf exists to close that gap. We bring intelligence-community discipline — a working knowledge of how adversaries operate and what they actually go after — to companies that can't staff a full security team but can't afford a breach either. That means testing your defenses the way an attacker would, building compliance that survives scrutiny, and watching your environment around the clock.

The name is the model. Direwolves hunted in packs and protected their own. We work the same way: not a vendor that drops a report and disappears, but an extension of your team. Hunt threats before they hunt you.

What we stand for

  • Straight answers — we tell you where you stand instead of scaring you into a contract.
  • Findings you can act on — prioritized and mapped to fixes, not buried in an appendix.
  • Compliance that holds up — documentation and evidence built to stand in front of an assessor, not just fill a binder.
  • Partnership over drive-by reports — we stay engaged through remediation, not just discovery.
  • 24/7 vigilance — managed detection and response that watches your environment while you run your business.

Meet the CEO

Mitchel Shepherd

Chief Executive Officer
10+ years in cybersecurity & intelligence operations Supported U.S. DoD intelligence missions Formal education in Cybersecurity & Intelligence Studies CMMC, NIST & DFARS specialist

Direwolf Cybersecurity is led by Chief Executive Officer Mitchel Shepherd, who brings over 10 years of experience in cybersecurity and intelligence operations. His background includes supporting United States Department of Defense intelligence missions and leading security and compliance initiatives in complex environments.

Mitch specializes in supporting Department of Defense contractors and manufacturers with CMMC, NIST, and DFARS requirements. His experience includes collaboration with the Manufacturing Extension Partnership network and multiple universities, enabling Direwolf Cybersecurity to deliver practical, defensible compliance solutions aligned with real-world operational needs.
