Defense supply chain

CUI & CMMC Are Home Turf

If you handle CUI or FCI for the Department of Defense, compliance isn't a side project — it's a condition of doing business. Direwolf specializes in moving manufacturing, defense, aerospace, and space companies from "we think we're covered" to a documented, defensible security posture.

The DoD suspended Phase 2 third-party assessments in July 2026, but the underlying rules never moved: DFARS 252.204-7012 and NIST 800-171 still govern your contracts, SPRS scores are still due, and inflated self-assessments still draw False Claims Act scrutiny. The pause is not a reprieve.
